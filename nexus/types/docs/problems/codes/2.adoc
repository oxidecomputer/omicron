== Description

One or more control plane services on the impacted sled have failed and will
not be automatically restarted.

== Impact

The unavailability of the impacted services may degrade some or all control
plane functionality.

The precise nature of the impact depends on the services that have failed. Some
services may have sufficient redundancy to prevent any user- or operator-visible
degradation, while others may prevent some or all control plane operation until
the active problem is repaired.

== Action Required

Contact Oxide support.

== Automated Response

None.
