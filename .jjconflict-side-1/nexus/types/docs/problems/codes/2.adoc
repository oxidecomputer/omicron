== Description

One or more control plane services on the impacted sled have failed and will
not be automatically restarted.

== Impact

The unavailability of the impacted services may degrade a variety of aspects of
system functionality.

The precise nature of the impact depends on the services that have failed.
Running instances, distributed disks, networking, and control plane functionality
may all be degraded or unavailable.

Some services may have sufficient redundancy to prevent any user- or
operator-visible impact. Other services may impact the availability of resources
on a particular sled, while resources on other sleds may remain remain
unaffected. The failure of networking services may result in complete or partial
loss of external network connectivity.

Typically, this active problem does not cause unrecoverable data loss.

== Action Required

Contact Oxide support.

== Automated Response

None.
